# JourneyDeck's origin

JourneyDeck began as an idea on **Friday, August 7, 2026, at 9:23 a.m.**, before it was called JourneyDeck or DriveOS.

Patrick Benjamin Stewart asked ChatGPT for a project that could combine his Windows 11 Pro laptop, ChatGPT, 1Password CLI, Spotify account, and Tesla. ChatGPT proposed a **Tesla Drive “Flight Recorder” + Music Journal**: a private record of drives that brings together routes, vehicle information, and the music played along the way.

That conversation is the earliest known record of the idea that became JourneyDeck. Patrick found the original conversation and supplied a screenshot confirming its displayed date and time on September 30, 2026. The screenshot displays “Fri, Aug 7 at 9:23 AM”; the year is identified from the project timeline and Patrick's confirmation. The displayed time has no explicit timezone, so this record does not assign one.
