# JourneyDeck Medallions

User-selected list recorded September 13, 2026. These are the ten medallions to create, in the requested order.

## Theme and color palette

Each medallion can use any of the app's four current themes and its corresponding color palette. Use the existing app themes and palettes as design references when creating the artwork.

## Medallions

1. **The First Track** — First journey
2. **Road Regular** — 10 journeys
3. **Century Road** — 100 miles
4. **Soundtrack 100** — 100 song plays
5. **After Dark** — First nighttime journey
6. **Explorer** — Five destinations
7. **Memory Maker** — First Memory created
8. **Open Road** — 50 journeys
9. **Thousand Mile Club** — 1,000 miles
10. **Grand Tourer** — 100 journeys

## Approved designs

- **The First Track** — Option 1, gold-vinyl sunrise and musical-staff road.
- **Road Regular** — Option 6, sculpted numeral 10 formed from parallel road bands, with the roadway rising through the zero.
- **Century Road** — Fresh-set Option 1, bold Art Deco 100 with streamlined road wings, a central approach road, and an MI plaque. Warm Ivory excludes blue and teal.
- **Soundtrack 100** — Option 1, a large vinyl record with 100 on its center label and a highway flowing into the record groove. Cinematic Dark is deep purple with burnt-orange and amber highlights rather than pink-dominant. Warm Ivory excludes blue and teal.
- **Memory Maker** — Option 8, two hands holding an instant-photo memory of an open road, mountains, and sunrise, with a small heart on the photo border. Cinematic Dark uses a high-glow neon treatment with black-purple, ultraviolet, orange, amber, and selective cyan; Warm Ivory excludes blue and teal.

Theme-specific color appears on the medallion front. The Minted coin body, edge, and reverse remain regular gold across all themes.
