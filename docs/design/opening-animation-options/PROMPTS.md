# Generated foundation prompts

Built-in image generation was used for the three portrait, text-free visual foundations. The existing JourneyDeck welcome and Statistics artwork were supplied as style references. The exact JourneyDeck icon, wordmark, catchphrase, watermark, and motion were added deterministically afterward by `build_previews.py`.

## Option 1 — The Road Awakens

Create a full-screen 9:19.5 portrait, cinematic night landscape viewed from a high aerial angle. One winding road should climb from the lower foreground toward a distant horizon. The road is mostly dark but carries restrained coral, magenta, violet, and cyan long-exposure light, with room for an animated route reveal and a calm dark logo area around 42% from the top. Use premium photographic realism, strong depth, fine haze, near-black edges, and the JourneyDeck reference palette. No text, logo, UI, phone frame, border, people, cars, hard vignette, generic cyberpunk, or watermark.

## Option 2 — Miles Become Memories

Create a full-screen 9:19.5 portrait cinematic night road seen through memory-like reflections. Three subtle floating photographic glass panes should sit along one connected coral route and show abstract road-trip moments: sunset water, city lights, and a starry overlook. They must feel like luminous reflections rather than interface cards. Preserve a calm dark logo area around 42% from the top. Use emotional, nostalgic photographic realism with near-black, deep aubergine, coral, violet, and restrained cyan. No text, labels, logo, UI chrome, phone frame, border, identifiable people, collage treatment, fake text, or watermark.

## Option 3 — Road Meets Soundtrack

Create a full-screen 9:19.5 portrait cinematic scene with a physically realistic black vinyl record in the lower half. Its fine grooves should flow outward and transform naturally into a winding coastal highway made of coral and violet long-exposure light. Suggest a faint audio waveform through reflected light rather than UI. Preserve calm dark space around 38% from the top for a logo. Use tactile, premium photographic realism with midnight navy, aubergine, coral, magenta, and subtle cyan. No text, logo, UI, phone frame, border, giant music notes, equalizer interface, nightclub imagery, generic cyberpunk, or watermark.
