**Approved:** August 31, 2026

## Interaction

- Tapping the compact **Manual** card selects Manual and expands it in place.
- The Automatic card simultaneously collapses into the compact comparison state.
- Tapping either method only selects and explains it; it does not advance onboarding.
- The selected checkmark moves with the selection.
- The bottom action changes to **Continue with Automatic** or **Continue with Manual**.
- Only the Continue action advances to Apple Music connection.
- Card expansion should animate smoothly in approximately 250–300 ms.

## Expanded Manual copy

**SELECTED · YOU’RE IN CONTROL**

**Manual Recording**

Tap Start when your journey begins and Finish when you’re done.

**Benefits**

- Every journey starts only when you choose.
- Lower background battery use.
- Apple Music can still add your soundtrack afterward.

**Limitations**

- Nothing before you tap Start can be recovered.
- You must remember to finish the journey.
- Only interact with the controls when it’s safe.

**Privacy**

Location recording runs only after you tap Start.
