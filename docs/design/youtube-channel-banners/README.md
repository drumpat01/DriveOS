# JourneyDeck YouTube banner options

Five upload-ready channel banner concepts, generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the approved JourneyDeck pulse icons as visual references. Each final JPEG is 2560 × 1440 (16:9), below 6 MB. The emblem and JourneyDeck name sit in the center safe strip; landscapes extend into areas YouTube may crop.

## Final prompt set

All five prompts requested one full-bleed 16:9 YouTube banner with a faithful circular JourneyDeck pulse emblem beside the exact word `JourneyDeck`. The emblem and full word were constrained to the central 60% of width and middle 29% of height, with no additional text, frame, watermark, or app mockup.

1. **Blue Hour Coast:** cinematic ocean-cliff touring road at blue hour; midnight navy and champagne light trails.
2. **Route Atlas:** luxurious abstract midnight-navy cartography and a fine champagne route with remembered-place points.
3. **Miles and Music:** city-at-night touring road, gold light trail, and a restrained rhythmic audio waveform.
4. **Sunrise Memories:** warm-ivory mountain road at dawn, sage hills, soft coral and plum emblem.
5. **Cinematic Neon:** black-plum night road toward a luminous horizon, coral and amber trails with ultraviolet haze.

The source renders were resized to 2560 × 1440 and encoded as quality-92 JPEGs with 4:4:4 chroma sampling. `review-sheet.jpg` is only a comparison preview; upload one of the five numbered files.

YouTube's [banner guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS&hl=en) recommend 2560 × 1440, centered safe-area content, and a file size of 6 MB or less.
