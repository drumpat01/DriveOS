# JourneyDeck final onboarding instructions

- **4a — Automatic:** reinforces that the journey starts and finishes automatically, and prompts the driver to play Apple Music through their iPhone or CarPlay.
- **4b — Manual:** shows the exact **Start Your Journey** action on Home and prominently reminds the driver to finish the journey after arriving.

Both variants end with **Let the Journey Begin**, which is intended to complete onboarding and open the Home tab.

The exact approved renders are now bundled into the mobile first-run flow. Publication status is tracked in `.ai/HANDOFF.md`.
