# Selected Home Start Control

The approved design is **Option 4 — Journey Portal**, revised to use JourneyDeck's orange-coral accent instead of green.

- Reference: `option-4-journey-portal-coral.png`
- SHA-256: `267FD51E921611702D16D5D81BB7C88879E8C8A83C2FE6BA37DE7D2028CDA2F5`
- Preserve the translucent dark-glass atmosphere and coral/pink illuminated bloom, feathering both fully into the photographic background. The only visible boundary is one thin coral-orange rounded neon outline with no opaque card fill or visible bloom boundary.
- Preserve the centered coral beacon, concentric rings, `READY` label, supporting copy, `Start Journey` label, and arrow.
- The translucent coral action haze fills the complete interior of the thin rounded outline, remains brightest behind `Start Journey`, and keeps a low-opacity edge wash so the road photograph remains visible.
- The haze is rounded by its own SVG rectangle; do not clip the top-level SVG view.
- The complete portal footprint is one Start Journey control. Every visible point throughout that area must activate the same action even though its visual edges are diffused.
- The control must expose one accessible button, not multiple nested touch targets.
- This combined portal applies to the ready/manual state. Recording and paused states retain their live metrics and dedicated actions.
