## Common brief

Use case: lighting-weather edit. Asset type: production raster header image for the JourneyDeck iPhone app warm ivory light theme. Edit the supplied image, keeping the same subject, camera angle, framing, object placement, road geometry and aspect ratio. Create a genuinely light, naturally illuminated version, not a white overlay or inverted negative. High-key warm ivory #fffaf0, champagne, soft peach/coral highlights and restrained dusty lilac shadows, realistic texture and crisp important details. Lift deep black surroundings into warm pale materials or softly lit scenery so the image sits naturally on an ivory page. Elegant cinematic photography with daylight softness. Preserve recognizability; do not add objects, logos, text, app interface, borders or watermarks. Retain the original wide full-bleed framing, about 16:9, no padding.
