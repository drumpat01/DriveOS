## Promotional text (170 max)

Ask your drives questions, relive every route, and keep the roads, songs and photos that matter, privately, on iPhone and iPad.

## What's New

JourneyDeck 4.0 is a new look and a lot more.
- Redesigned for iPhone and iPad with native navigation: Today, Memories, Soundtrack and Atlas.
- Ask JourneyDeck: ask about your drives, music and places in plain language.
- Memories with your own photos, and a map of the roads you've driven.
- New Grand Touring design, plus Autumn Drive and Aurora Glass themes.
- Start a Journey widget, and Siri shortcuts for recording.
- iPad now shows your full library. Record on iPhone; it syncs through your private iCloud.

## Description

Every drive has a story. Keep yours with JourneyDeck.

JourneyDeck is a private driving journal for the roads you travel, the music you hear and the memories you want to keep. Record a journey on your iPhone, then revisit its route, distance, time and soundtrack on iPhone and iPad.

REMEMBER EVERY ROAD
Start and finish journeys from your iPhone, from the Start a Journey widget, from Apple Watch, or by asking Siri. Drop a marker with a note and photos at any point along the way. Recording is local first, so your route is saved without a server connection. Tesla owners can connect Tessie to record automatically (JourneyDeck Plus). [VERIFY: Tessie/automatic recording wording]

RELIVE THE JOURNEY
Watch your route unfold in a smooth replay with the songs that played along the way. Browse everything in a layout designed for iPhone and for iPad.

ASK JOURNEYDECK
Ask "What was my top artist this month?" or "How far did I drive last week?" Answers come from your archive on your device, using Apple Intelligence when your device supports it. Ask from the app or with Siri. Requires JourneyDeck Plus. [VERIFY: Apple Intelligence availability wording]

HEAR YOUR ROADTRIP SOUNDTRACK
Connect Apple Music to build a soundtrack from your authorized listening history. Some song locations may be estimated. You can also tap Identify Song to recognize a track with ShazamKit; microphone access is used only for that request and audio is never saved.

TURN DRIVES INTO MEMORIES
Bring related journeys together in a Memory with your own photos and notes. Earn collectible medallions as you reach milestones. [VERIFY: medallion count; the old text said ten]

DISCOVER YOUR ROAD STORY
Atlas shows your miles, routes, places, driving rhythms and soundtracks, calculated on your device. Track the U.S. states you have driven. Atlas requires JourneyDeck Plus.

CHOOSE YOUR OWN LOOK
Six themes with matching app icons: Grand Touring and Warm Ivory are free; Cinematic Dark, Rosewater, Autumn Drive and Aurora Glass come with JourneyDeck Plus.

YOUR ARCHIVE, KEPT PRIVATE
Your archive is stored on your device first. Optional private iCloud sync keeps your library available across your iPhone and iPad using your Apple Account. Sign-in, music permissions and iCloud sync are optional. Set private Home, Work and School locations and create share cards with sensitive-place protection.

JOURNEYDECK PLUS
Every new install includes a 3-day JourneyDeck Plus trial. After that, JourneyDeck stays free for recording and shows today's journeys. An auto-renewable membership (weekly or annual, with a free trial for eligible new subscribers) unlocks your complete history, Atlas, Ask JourneyDeck, premium themes and icons, and optional Tessie and Claude connections. Older journeys are never deleted when a membership ends. Restore Purchases is on the membership screen. [VERIFY: trial and free-tier wording against App Store offers]

iPad shows and syncs your library but does not record; recording needs an iPhone. Apple Watch controls recording on your paired iPhone and does not record routes independently. JourneyDeck is not a navigation app. Set up recording before driving and do not interact with the app while operating a vehicle.
