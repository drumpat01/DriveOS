# Applied Settings patterns

- Lead with profile and membership state, then organize navigation by user intent.
- Use compact 44–68 pt grouped rows instead of treating every destination as a feature card.
- Show the current value in the row when it helps the next decision.
- Keep one visual treatment for icons, dividers, chevrons, and section labels.
- Put Markers beside other journey-management tools rather than promoting it above all settings.
- Make Appearance an overview. Theme and app-icon galleries are separate drill-down screens.
- Keep detail screens focused on the task and replace large explanatory cards with concise footnotes or inset notes.
- Keep sign-out and deletion visually isolated at the end of Account & iCloud.
- Reserve enough bottom clearance for the floating tab bar on the root screen.
- Preserve JourneyDeck's local-first privacy, persistence, account, iCloud, music, and saved-place behavior.
