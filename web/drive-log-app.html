How logging a drive works

Tap Start Journey

Open JourneyDeck on your iPhone and tap "Start Journey" when you set off. That tap is what starts the log.

No surprise recordings

For everyone except Tesla owners, nothing records unless you start it: every drive begins with your own tap on Start Journey.

Cost

JourneyDeck is free to record. JourneyDeck Plus is $39.99/yr, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Logging drives in a Tesla

JourneyDeck works with Teslas through your iPhone. Tesla owners are the one exception to tapping Start Journey: JourneyDeck can detect Tesla drives automatically. Learn more on the Tesla drive journal page.

Your drive, with its soundtrack

JourneyDeck matches your Apple Music listening history to the route you drove. Learn more on the Apple Music soundtracks page.

FAQ

Is JourneyDeck free?

Yes, JourneyDeck is free to record. JourneyDeck Plus costs $39.99 a year and comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Does JourneyDeck record drives automatically?

No, not in general. You tap "Start Journey" to begin recording, and nothing records unless you start it. The only exception is for Tesla owners: JourneyDeck can detect Tesla drives automatically.

What devices does JourneyDeck run on?

JourneyDeck works on iPhone and is available on the App Store.
