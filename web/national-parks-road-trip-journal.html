MEMORIES / THE PARKS

Keep the days between the parks.

Start a journey for each leg, then group them into one Memory with photos and notes. Core recording works offline.

Long days, short moments

National park trips are long days of driving between short moments you really want to keep. Start a journey for each leg, and JourneyDeck saves the route, miles and drive time. Group the legs into one Memory with photos and notes, so Zion, Bryce and the gas station with the good jerky all live together.

Offline in the parks

Lots of parks have little or no signal. Core recording works offline and stays on your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Does it track my national parks trip automatically?

No. Nothing runs in the background. You start it. You stop it.

Does it need cell service?

No. Lots of parks have little or no signal, and core recording works offline on your iPhone.

Can I keep several park days together?

Start a journey for each leg, and JourneyDeck saves the route, miles and drive time. Group the legs into one Memory with photos and notes.
