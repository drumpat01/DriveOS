Common questions in JourneyDeck 4.0

Why can’t I record on iPad?

Recording needs an iPhone. iPad shows your library and receives journeys from your iPhone through your private iCloud.

What is the sample library, and how do I leave it?

The sample is a fictional library created on your device so you can explore JourneyDeck. It is never uploaded or synced. Turn it on or leave it in Settings > Account. Leaving returns you to your own library; the sample stays prepared on the device.

What is included with JourneyDeck Plus?

Every new install gets a 3-day Plus trial. After the trial, you can keep recording for free and view today’s journeys. A weekly or annual subscription unlocks full history, Atlas, Ask JourneyDeck, premium themes and icons, Tessie, and AI assistant connections. Older journeys are never deleted when the trial or membership ends. Restore Purchases is in Settings > Membership.

How much does Plus cost?

US App Store pricing is US$0.99 per week or US$24.99 per year, checked October 7, 2026. Prices may vary by region and can change. Your local price and terms appear before purchase. Paid subscriptions renew automatically until canceled in your Apple account settings. The 3-day Plus trial starts on a new install; purchasing a subscription is optional.

What is Identify Song?

Tap Identify Song to recognize a song playing around you with Apple’s ShazamKit. The microphone listens briefly for that request, then stops. Audio is never saved. This is separate from Apple Music listening-history matching and is included in the free experience. Use it when safely parked.

What is Tessie? Do I need a Tesla?

Tessie is a separate service for Tesla owners. With JourneyDeck Plus and your own Tessie access, you can import Tesla drive routes and charging history. Any Tessie fees are separate. You do not need Tessie or a Tesla to record journeys with your iPhone.

How do I disconnect an AI assistant?

Open Settings > AI Assistants > Connected assistants, then choose Disconnect for that assistant. You can also change its sharing switches there.

Why are items still arriving from iCloud?

The first iCloud sync on a new device can take a few minutes. JourneyDeck may say items are still arriving while it receives the rest of your library.

Before you write

  • Describe the screen or feature where the issue occurred.
  • Include your iPhone or iPad model, operating-system version, and JourneyDeck version if you can.
  • Do not include passwords, access tokens, or precise home/work addresses in an email.
  • For a privacy or deletion request, tell us which JourneyDeck profile or account is affected.

Privacy and account help

JourneyDeck is local first. You can manage most location, music, microphone, photo, and iCloud permissions directly in iOS Settings. You can also delete a JourneyDeck profile in the app. For help that cannot be completed on your iPhone, contact us by email.

Read the JourneyDeck Privacy Policy for more information about how the app handles journey and location data.
