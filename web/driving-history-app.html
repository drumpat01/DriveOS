A personal drive log

If you searched for a driving history app or a drive log app, JourneyDeck keeps a journal you start by hand. You decide which journeys to record. The archive starts on your iPhone.

How a drive gets into your history

  1. Download JourneyDeck and allow Location when you want route recording.
  2. Tap Start Journey before you set off. If you own a Tesla, JourneyDeck can detect your drives automatically.
  3. When parked, end the journey. Explore the route on your device.
  4. Optionally connect Apple Music for a soundtrack, or group drives into a Memory.

What you can look back on

  • Route, distance, and drive time for journeys you recorded.
  • An optional soundtrack matched from your Apple Music listening history.
  • Free to record. Plus is $39.99/yr, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a driving history app?

A private history of the roads you choose to keep: routes, places, and the songs that played. All on your iPhone.

Is this the same as my phone's built-in location history?

No. JourneyDeck is a separate iPhone driving journal. You tap Start Journey when you want to keep a drive, then reopen that journal on your device.

Is JourneyDeck a DMV record or mileage tax log?

No. JourneyDeck is a personal drive journal, not a DMV driving record, not an insurance report, and not a mileage tax log.

Does it log every drive automatically?

No. You tap Start Journey when you want to keep a drive. The only exception is for Tesla owners: JourneyDeck can detect Tesla drives automatically.

Can I look back at older drives?

Yes, you can reopen the journeys you recorded on your iPhone. Recording is free. JourneyDeck Plus is an optional annual membership, and the app shows what it includes before you subscribe.

How much is Plus?

Free to record. Plus is $39.99/yr, with a 7-day free trial.
