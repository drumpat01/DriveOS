Your drive, your music

Record drives on iPhone with an optional Apple Music soundtrack

JourneyDeck is an iPhone app that records drives and builds an optional soundtrack from your authorized Apple Music listening history. Start a journey before you drive, then revisit its route and the songs JourneyDeck matched to it. Recording is manual and local first; Apple Music is optional.

THE SOUNDTRACK / JOURNEYDECK 2.0

The songs beside the road.

See the soundtrack saved with a journey in the released iPhone app. Connect Apple Music for optional matching, or use Identify Song yourself when a track catches your ear.

Identify Song uses ShazamKit when you ask it to listen; JourneyDeck does not keep an audio recording.

How it works

  1. Tap Start Journey before setting off. JourneyDeck records your route, distance, and time on your iPhone.
  2. If you connect Apple Music, JourneyDeck checks music playing during the journey and recent listening history to match available songs to the drive. Some song times and locations may be approximate, and a song may not always be found.
  3. End the journey when parked. Revisit the route and saved soundtrack, or add the drive to a Memory with photos and notes.

Your archive starts on your device

Route recording works without Apple Music, an account, or a server connection. Sign-in and private iCloud sync are optional. Read the JourneyDeck privacy policy for details.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app that records drives and builds a soundtrack from Apple Music?

Yes. JourneyDeck records journeys you start on your iPhone and can match songs from your authorized Apple Music listening history to each recorded drive. It does not guarantee a match for every song.

Do I need Apple Music to record a drive?

No. Route recording works without Apple Music. An active Apple Music subscription is needed for its automatic soundtrack option; manual song recognition is another optional method.

Is JourneyDeck a navigation app?

No. Set up recording before driving and do not interact with the app while operating a vehicle.
