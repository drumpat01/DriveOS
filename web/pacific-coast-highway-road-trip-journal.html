Keep the coast.

The view, then the roll

Highway 1 is the kind of drive where you pull over every ten minutes because the view got even better. Then you get home and can't remember which cove was which.

How to keep the drive

Tap Start Journey before Big Sur, put the phone down and drive. JourneyDeck saves the route, distance and drive time on your iPhone. Cell signal disappears for long stretches along that coast, and core recording still works offline.

Add your Bixby Bridge photos and a note to a Memory. Connect Apple Music if you want the playlist kept with the drive.

Private, on your phone

It's not navigation and it won't plan your stops. It just keeps the trip. Private, on your phone, no account.

Frequently asked questions

Does it track my Pacific Coast Highway trip automatically?

No. You tap Start Journey when you set off and end it when you park.

Does it need cell service?

No. Signal disappears for long stretches along the coast, and core recording still works offline.

Is it a navigation app?

No. It won't plan your stops. It just keeps the trip.
