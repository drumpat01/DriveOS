How the Tesla connection works

  1. In JourneyDeck, open Settings (the profile button on Today), then Music & Connections, and tap Connect on the Tesla card. This needs Plus.
  2. In the Tesla service you already use, open Settings, then Developer, tap Generate Access Token, and copy it.
  3. Paste it into the access token field in JourneyDeck and tap Connect.

That service is separate, and any fees for it are separate. The token stays in this iPhone's Keychain.

Without that connection, you start a drive yourself. One tap or Siri. Those are the Start Journey and Stop Journey shortcuts.

Frequently asked questions

What happens without the Tesla connection?

You start each drive yourself, with one tap or Siri. Automatic history needs Plus.

What does the iPhone detect?

With Plus, the iPhone's GPS detects your drives automatically and imports the car's drives and charging history from the last 30 days.

Do I need a Tesla?

No. You can record on an iPhone without a Tesla. The Tesla connection is optional, for owners who already have one.

What iPhone does it need?

iOS 17 or later.

How much is Plus?

JourneyDeck Plus, $39.99/yr, 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
