The trip, later

I built JourneyDeck so a long drive would still be there when I got home. With Tessie and Plus, the iPhone's GPS detects the drives, and Tessie imports the car's drives and charging history from the last 30 days. Then you can replay the whole trip.

That's automatic drive and charging history with Tessie, a Plus feature. It needs both. iPhone only.

How the Tessie connection works

  1. In JourneyDeck, open Settings (the profile button on Today), then Music & Connections, and tap Connect Tessie on the Tessie card. This needs Plus.
  2. In the Tessie app, open Settings, then Developer, tap Generate Access Token, and copy it.
  3. Paste it into the Tessie access token field in JourneyDeck and tap Connect Tessie.

Tessie is a separate service, and any Tessie fees are separate. The token stays in this iPhone's Keychain.

Without Tessie, you start a drive yourself. One tap or Siri. Those are the Start Journey and Stop Journey shortcuts.

Frequently asked questions

What happens without Tessie?

You start a drive yourself. One tap or Siri. Those are the Start Journey and Stop Journey shortcuts. Automatic drive and charging history needs Plus and Tessie.

What does the iPhone detect?

The iPhone's GPS detects drives. Tessie turns on automatic drive detection and imports the car's drives and charging history from the last 30 days. That needs Plus and Tessie, and it runs on iPhone.

Do I need a Tesla?

No. You can record on an iPhone without a Tesla. Tessie is optional, for Tesla owners who already use it.

What iPhone does it need?

iOS 17 or later.

How much is Plus?

JourneyDeck Plus, $39.99/yr, 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
