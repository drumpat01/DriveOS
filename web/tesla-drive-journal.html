How it works

  1. Download JourneyDeck on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Start when you want to keep a drive.
  3. Tesla owners can connect Tessie with Plus. Drives and charging are recorded automatically, instead of tapping Start. JourneyDeck tracks charging and efficiency.

Tessie is a separate service, and any Tessie fees are separate.

Plus

Plus unlocks your full history. Tesla owners can connect Tessie so drives and charging record automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What iPhone does it need?

iOS 17 or later.

How much is Plus?

Free to record. Plus is $39.99/year, with a 7-day free trial.
