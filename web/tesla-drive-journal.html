THE ROAD / TESLA

Drives, without tapping Start.

Connect Tessie with Plus and JourneyDeck records the drive and the charge for you. It also tracks charging and efficiency.

Who it is for

Tesla and EV owners who want a private journal of the drives they take, and the music that was playing.

You do not need a Tesla to keep that journal. Tessie is the extra step for Tesla owners.

How it works

  1. Download JourneyDeck. It is an iPhone app for iOS 17 or later.
  2. Recording is free. Tap Start when you want to keep a drive, and the music you played stays with it.
  3. If you own a Tesla, connect Tessie with Plus. Drives and charging are recorded automatically, instead of tapping Start.
  4. Plus tracks charging and efficiency.

Plus

It is free to record. Plus is $0.99 a week or $39.99 a year.
