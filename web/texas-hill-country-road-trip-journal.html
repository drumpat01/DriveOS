THE ROAD / HILL COUNTRY

Keep the drive.

Tap Start Journey when you leave the house and JourneyDeck keeps the route, the miles and the drive time on your iPhone.

Close, for Texas

I live in DFW, so the Hill Country is our "close" road trip. Close in Texas means about four hours.

How to keep the drive

Fredericksburg, Enchanted Rock, the bluebonnets if you time it right in spring. Start a journey when you back out of the driveway and forget about it until you're eating brisket. Add photos and a note to a Memory. Keep the Apple Music soundtrack too.

Private and local first

It's private and local first. No account, no subscription for core recording, and nothing records unless you start it.

Frequently asked questions

Does it track my Hill Country trip automatically?

Nothing records unless you start it. Tap Start Journey when you leave the house.

Do I need an account?

No. It's private and local first. No account, no subscription for core recording.

Is it a guide?

No. It won't plan your stops or find the best peach stand. It just keeps the drive.

How much is Plus?

Free to record. Plus is $39.99/yr, with a 7-day free trial.
