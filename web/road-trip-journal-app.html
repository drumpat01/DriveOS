THE ROAD / JOURNEYDECK 4.0

Every journey, kept.

Manually start a journey on iPhone to save your route, distance, and drive time on your device. No account required. Optional Apple Music soundtracks and Memories with photos and notes sit with the road.

Who it is for

Drivers who want a private archive of the roads they take, everyday drives and longer trips, without signing up for a cloud tracker or handing over a continuous trip history account.

How it works

  1. Download JourneyDeck and allow Location when you want route recording.
  2. Tap Start Journey on Home (or use Siri, the Start a Journey widget, or Apple Watch). Put the phone away; the route keeps recording while locked.
  3. When parked, end the journey. Explore the route on your device.
  4. Optionally connect Apple Music for a soundtrack, or group drives into a Memory with photos and notes.

What stays with each trip

  • The route, distance, and drive time you recorded.
  • Places along the drive, including Home, Work, or School labels you choose to save.
  • Photos and notes you add to a Memory or a Journey Marker.
  • An optional soundtrack from your authorized Apple Music listening history, or Identify Song with ShazamKit. Audio is never saved.

What private and local-first mean

  • Core recording works offline, without an account or subscription.
  • Journey data starts on your iPhone and remains there until you delete it, delete the relevant profile, or remove the app. Private iCloud continuity is optional.
  • Sign in with Apple is optional.
  • Journeys never start automatically. You start and finish intentionally.

Frequently asked questions

What is a road trip journal app?

A road trip journal app keeps a personal archive of trips you choose to record. JourneyDeck is a private driving journal for iPhone: you tap Start Journey before you set off, and your route, distance, and drive time are saved on your device.

Is JourneyDeck a road trip log or car trip log app?

Yes, as a manual journal. You start and finish each drive yourself. It is not an automatic mileage tracker or a cloud trip log that records every drive in the background.

Does JourneyDeck need an account?

No. Core recording works without an account or subscription. Sign in with Apple and private iCloud sync are optional.

Does it record every trip automatically?

No. You tap Start Journey when you want to keep a drive.

Is JourneyDeck a navigation app?

No. Set up recording before driving and do not interact with the app while operating a vehicle.
