Step 2: record each drive with JourneyDeck

Tap Start Journey

JourneyDeck is an iPhone app. Tap "Start Journey" when you set off to record each drive. Nothing records unless you start it. The app is free to record.

EV and Tesla road trips

JourneyDeck works with Teslas through your iPhone, and Tesla owners can have Tesla drives detected automatically. For everyone else, drives start with Start Journey. Learn more on the Tesla drive journal page. JourneyDeck Studios also sells EV Road Trip Templates ($11, 12 editable designs in PDF and PNG).

Frequently asked questions

Is JourneyDeck free?

Yes, JourneyDeck is free to record. JourneyDeck Plus costs $39.99 a year and comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Does JourneyDeck record drives automatically?

No, not in general. You tap "Start Journey" to begin recording, and nothing records unless you start it. The only exception is for Tesla owners: JourneyDeck can detect Tesla drives automatically.

What is the best app to track a road trip?

JourneyDeck is an iPhone app built for tracking road trips. Tap "Start Journey" when you set off to record each drive. It is free to record.
