YOUR ROADS, YOUR ARCHIVE

Private driving journal for iPhone

JourneyDeck is a private driving journal for iPhone. You manually start a journey before you set off; JourneyDeck saves your route, distance, and drive time on your device as a local-first archive you can reopen later. Optional soundtracks and Memories with photos and notes sit with the road. It is not a navigation app or an automatic cloud trip log.

Download on the App Store ↗

Who it’s for

Drivers who want a private archive of the roads they take, everyday drives and longer trips, without signing up for a cloud tracker or handing over a continuous trip history account.

Looking for a road trip journal app, car trip log, or trip journal on iPhone? That overview stays with the same private, local-first recorder.

If you need a personal driving history app or drive log, that page explains the journal, and that it is not a DMV record or mileage tax log.

Planning a specific drive? There are journals for Route 66, the Pacific Coast Highway, national parks, and Texas Hill Country.

How it works

  1. Download JourneyDeck and allow Location when you want route recording.
  2. Tap Start Journey on Home (or use Siri or Apple Watch). Put the phone away; the route keeps recording while locked.
  3. When parked, end the journey. Explore the route on your device.
  4. Optionally connect Apple Music for a soundtrack, or group drives into a Memory with photos and notes. See the travel journal app with photos.

What private and local-first mean

  • Core recording works offline, without an account or subscription.
  • Journey data starts on your iPhone and remains there until you delete it, delete the relevant profile, or remove the app. Private iCloud continuity is optional.
  • Sign in with Apple is optional.
  • Journeys never start automatically. You start and finish intentionally.

Read the JourneyDeck privacy policy for details.

How it compares

  • Automatic mileage and trip trackers: background logging. JourneyDeck is a manual journal. See the road trip tracker app overview for how route recording works.
  • Cloud travel diaries: a synced account by default. JourneyDeck’s archive starts on your device.
  • Navigation: JourneyDeck is for recording and looking back. Set up before driving; don’t interact while driving.

Frequently asked questions

What is a private driving journal app for iPhone?

An app that lets you keep a personal archive of drives and routes on your phone. JourneyDeck is a private, local-first driving journal for iPhone: you manually start a journey, and your route is saved on your device.

Does JourneyDeck need an account?

No. Core recording works without an account or subscription. Sign in with Apple and private iCloud sync are optional.

Is this the same as Journey.Cloud or the tarot “Journey Deck”?

No. JourneyDeck is the iPhone driving journal at journeydeck.me and App Store ID 6806502526.

Does it record every drive automatically?

No. You tap Start Journey when you want to keep a drive.

How much is Plus?
