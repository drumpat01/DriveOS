How soundtrack matching works ↗
02 / IDENTIFY SONG · FREE

Keep the song you hear.

Identify Song is a button in JourneyDeck that uses Apple’s ShazamKit to recognize a song playing around you. It uses your microphone briefly after you tap, then stops listening. Audio is never saved. Use it when safely parked.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is JourneyDeck free?

Yes, JourneyDeck is free to record. JourneyDeck Plus costs $39.99 a year and comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Does JourneyDeck record drives automatically?

No, not in general. You tap "Start Journey" to begin recording, and nothing records unless you start it. The only exception is for Tesla owners: JourneyDeck can detect Tesla drives automatically.

Does JourneyDeck work with Tesla?

Yes. JourneyDeck works with Teslas through your iPhone. Learn more at journeydeck.me/tesla-drive-journal.

What devices does JourneyDeck run on?

JourneyDeck works on iPhone and is available on the App Store.

Is there a discount on JourneyDeck Plus?

New subscribers can use code ROADTRIP30 to get JourneyDeck Plus annual for $29.99 for the first year. The code expires Dec 1, 2026 and is limited to 500 uses.

What is the best app to track a road trip?

JourneyDeck is an iPhone app built for tracking road trips. Tap "Start Journey" when you set off to record each drive. It is free to record.

Who makes JourneyDeck?

JourneyDeck is made by JourneyDeck Studios.
