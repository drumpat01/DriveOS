YOUR FIRST JOURNEY

Three steps.
A story of your own.

Start with a drive you were going to take anyway.

  1. 01

    Make yourself at home.

    Download JourneyDeck, open the app, and allow Location access. Connect Apple Music if you’d like a soundtrack.

    SET UP
  2. 02

    Start before you set off.

    Tap Start Journey on Home, ask Siri, or use your paired Apple Watch. Put your phone away and enjoy the drive. Your route keeps recording while the phone is locked.

    GO
  3. 03

    Arrive. Save. Remember.

    Once you’re parked, end the journey. Explore your route and songs, then bring your favorite drives and photos into a Memory.

    KEEP
