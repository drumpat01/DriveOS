A Travel Journal App for iPhone, Built for the Road

JourneyDeck is an iPhone app that keeps a record of your drives. Tap "Start Journey" to begin recording a trip, and unless you own a Tesla, nothing is recorded until you do. Recording is free, and JourneyDeck Plus is an optional $39.99 a year plan with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Road trips in a Tesla

JourneyDeck works with Teslas through your iPhone. Tesla owners are the one exception to starting by hand: JourneyDeck can detect Tesla drives automatically. Everyone else taps "Start Journey". Learn more on the Tesla drive journal page.

Pair it with a trip planner

Plan the trip in a planner, then use JourneyDeck to keep a record of the drive. JourneyDeck Studios also offers a free Notion trip planner.

Your drive, with its soundtrack

FAQ

Is JourneyDeck free?

Yes, JourneyDeck is free to record. JourneyDeck Plus costs $39.99 a year and comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Does JourneyDeck record drives automatically?

No, not in general. You tap "Start Journey" to begin recording, and nothing records unless you start it. The only exception is for Tesla owners: JourneyDeck can detect Tesla drives automatically.

What devices does JourneyDeck run on?

JourneyDeck works on iPhone and is available on the App Store.

Who makes JourneyDeck?

JourneyDeck is made by JourneyDeck Studios.
